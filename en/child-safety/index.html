Child Safety Standards

Yeonlink app · Operated by ELMO COMPANY · Effective 16 November 2026 (English page published 28 November 2026)

Yeonlink is a dating app for adults only: 18 and over, and 19 and over in the Republic of Korea. We do not tolerate child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) in any form.

1. What is prohibited

  • Uploading, sending, requesting or keeping child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Any text, photo or conversation that sexualises a minor
  • Approaching or grooming a minor for a sexual purpose, or asking a minor to meet or to send images
  • Arranging prostitution involving a minor, sextortion, threats or demands for money
  • Use of the app by minors. An account opened with a false age is closed as soon as we find it.

2. How we prevent it

  • Date of birth is checked at sign-up, in the app and again on our servers. Anyone under 18 (under 19 in Korea) cannot join.
  • Automatic filters screen profiles, answers, notes and messages for sexual language and banned terms.
  • Photos are shared only after two members have exchanged six messages and both agree.
  • A member reported by several people is hidden automatically until our review is finished.

3. How to report

  • In the app: on a profile or in a conversation, open the menu at the top right, choose Report and pick a reason. The person is blocked at once and is not told who reported them.
  • By email: help@yeonlink.com. You do not need to be a member.

We review reports within 24 hours. Reports about child safety are handled first.

4. What we do

  • When a violation is confirmed we remove the content and permanently ban the account.
  • Where we suspect CSAM or a crime against a minor, we report it to the police or other competent authority as the applicable law requires, and cooperate with the investigation.
  • We comply with applicable child safety laws, including the Korean Act on the Protection of Children and Youth against Sex Offenses and the Act on Promotion of Information and Communications Network Utilization and Information Protection.
