Safety Policy

Yeonlink app · Operated by ELMO COMPANY · Effective 28 November 2026

This policy applies to every Yeonlink member. It is also the safety policy required for members in Colorado.

1. Prohibited content and conduct

  • Threatening, harassing, stalking or intimidating anyone, or encouraging violence or self-harm
  • Any sexual conduct with another person without that person's consent, and any attempt to arrange it
  • Sexual content involving, or sexual approaches to, anyone under 18 (see our child safety standards)
  • Sharing or threatening to share intimate images without consent; sextortion; trafficking or prostitution
  • Using someone else's photos or identity, or misstating your age
  • Asking members for money, promoting investments or gambling, or any other scam
  • Hate speech, and sexual or violent content in profiles, answers, notes or messages
  • Recording, screenshotting or sharing conversations or photos without the other person's agreement

2. Criminal background screening

Yeonlink does not conduct criminal background screening on any member, at sign-up or afterwards. Because we do not screen, we do not exclude members on the basis of a criminal conviction of any type unless it comes to our attention through a report or a request from law enforcement, in which case we review the account under section 4.

3. Identity and age

Yeonlink does not verify a member's identity. Members state their date of birth when they sign up; the app and our servers refuse anyone under 18 (under 19 in the Republic of Korea). We do not check identity documents. In the Republic of Korea we may additionally require mobile-carrier identity verification. An account found to belong to a minor is closed.

4. Suspending and barring members

  • We review every report within 24 hours. Reports about the safety of a child or about violence are handled first.
  • Depending on seriousness we issue a warning, suspend the account for a period, or bar the member permanently. Sexual misconduct, threats, content sexualising minors and scams lead to a permanent bar.
  • A member reported by several different members is hidden from others automatically until we finish the review.
  • When you report someone, they are blocked from you immediately and are not told who reported them.

5. Appeals

A member who has been suspended or barred may appeal by writing to help@yeonlink.com from the email address on the account within 30 days, explaining why the decision was wrong. We review the appeal and reply within 14 days. A bar for conduct involving minors is not reversed unless the report is shown to be mistaken.

6. Notice to other members

Yeonlink does not currently notify members who were in contact with a reported or barred member. When a member is barred, their answers, links and conversations disappear from other members' screens. If law enforcement asks us to help contact members who may have been affected by a crime, we cooperate as the law allows.

7. How to report

  • In the app: open the menu at the top right of a profile or conversation, choose Report, and pick a reason. You can add a note.
  • By email: help@yeonlink.com. You do not need an account to write to us.
  • If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (911 in the United States).

Report honestly. Do not make a report that you know to be false, or that is made out of malice or bias against a person's race, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, disability or other protected characteristic. Abusing the reporting system is itself a violation and may lead to suspension.

8. Consent

Engaging in sexual conduct with another person without that person's consent is a violation of this policy and is against the law. It may result in criminal and civil liability.

9. Resources

If you or someone you know has experienced sexual assault, domestic violence, stalking or another crime:

  • Emergency: 911
  • National Sexual Assault Hotline (RAINN): 1-800-656-4673 · rainn.org
  • National Domestic Violence Hotline: 1-800-799-7233 · thehotline.org
  • Colorado Coalition Against Sexual Assault: ccasa.org
  • Violence Free Colorado: violencefreecolorado.org
  • Colorado Crisis Services: 1-844-493-8255 · coloradocrisisservices.org
  • Outside the United States, contact your local emergency number and local victim-support services.

10. Safety measures we take

  • Photos stay hidden from everyone until two members have exchanged six messages and both agree to share them. The server, not only the app, enforces this.
  • Your exact location is never stored or shown. It is rounded to about 1 km, and others see only an approximate distance.
  • Automatic filters screen profiles, answers, notes and messages for sexual and abusive language.
  • You can block or report any member at any time, turn location off, hide your answers, and delete your account in the app.
  • Adults only: date of birth is checked at sign-up and under-age accounts are closed.
  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.

Dating safely. Keep your surname, address and workplace out of your profile. Never send money to someone you met online. Meet in a public place for the first time, tell someone you trust where you are going, arrange your own transport, and leave if anything feels wrong.
