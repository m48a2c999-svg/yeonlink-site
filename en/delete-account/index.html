Delete your account and data

Yeonlink app · Operated by ELMO COMPANY

You can delete your Yeonlink account and the data in it at any time, free of charge, in either of two ways.

Option 1. Delete it in the app

  1. Open Yeonlink and tap the Me tab at the bottom.
  2. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  3. Read the notice and confirm. The account is deleted immediately and cannot be restored.

Option 2. Ask by email

If you cannot use the app, write to help@yeonlink.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". After confirming it is you, we delete the account within 7 days and tell you when it is done.

You can also ask us at the same address to delete only part of your data (a profile photo or a particular answer, for example) while keeping the account. Profile photos and your introduction can be removed or changed directly in the Me tab.

What is kept for a limited period, then deleted

To comply with the law and to prevent abuse, only the following is kept separately for the period shown and then destroyed.

DataHow long
Records of account restrictions, to prevent abuse6 months after you leave
Reports and how they were handled1 year after handling is complete
Access logs3 months (Korean Protection of Communications Secrets Act)
Contract and payment records, if you subscribed5 years (Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
Records confirming the use and provision of location dataThe period set in the Location Services terms

If you have a subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription in the store. Cancel it yourself in the subscription settings of the store you paid through (App Store or Google Play).
