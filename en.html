2. Why we handle it

  1. To identify members, to confirm you meet the minimum age, and to prevent duplicate or fraudulent accounts
  2. To provide the question of the day and show you answers from members near you
  3. To provide links, conversations, and photo sharing between members who both consent
  4. To send notifications about new links, connections, messages and the daily question, if you turn them on. Notification text never contains the content of messages or answers.
  5. To show Plus members who read their answer
  6. To handle reports, restrict accounts, and respond to disputes
  7. To produce usage statistics in a form that cannot identify an individual

3. How long we keep it

When you delete your account we destroy your data without delay, except for the following, which we keep for a set period and then destroy.

  • Records of account restrictions, to prevent abuse: 6 months after withdrawal
  • Reports and how they were handled: 1 year after the matter is closed
  • Access logs: 3 months, as required by Korean law
  • Contract and payment records: 5 years, as required by Korean consumer-protection law
  • Records confirming use and provision of location data: as set out in the Location-Based Services Terms

4. What other members can see

Other members see your nickname, age, occupation, approximate area and distance (for example “within 1 km”, “about 3 km”), your answer of the day, and the interests you share with them. Photos are visible only after a link is connected, at least six messages have been exchanged, and both of you agree. Your exact location is never shown to anyone.

Plus members can see a list of people who read their answer that day. If you turn on “Browse without leaving a trace” under Me › Answer visibility, your views are not recorded, and they are never visible to anyone you have blocked.

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties, except where the law requires it or a law-enforcement authority requests it through a lawful procedure.

9. How we destroy data

Data whose retention period has ended, or whose purpose has been met, is destroyed without delay. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and photos are deleted from the underlying storage as well.

10. Your choices

You can view, correct or delete your data, or ask us to stop handling it, at any time. Edit it yourself under Me › Edit profile, or close your account under Me › Account › Delete account. You can withdraw photo-sharing consent at any time by disconnecting a link, and location consent under My location › Turn location off. You may also write to the support address below.

2. What we do with location

With your permission we use your device's current location to provide:

  1. An approximate distance to other members (for example “within 1 km”, “about 3 km”)
  2. Answers sorted by proximity, within the range you choose (a distance, or your whole country)
  3. A nearby radar, where distance is shown in steps of about 1 km and direction is deliberately blurred
  4. A count of how many members near you answered the question of the day, grouped by distance band, without showing who they are or where they are

We never give another member your exact location.

3. What we keep, and for how long

We store only your location rounded to about 1 km, and only to calculate distance. Each new reading overwrites the previous one. If you withdraw location consent or delete your account, it is destroyed immediately.

4. Records of use and provision

Records confirming the use and provision of location data are logged automatically by the location system and kept for six months.

5. Your rights

  1. You can withdraw location consent in whole or in part at any time. Distance features then switch off and answers are sorted by shared interests instead.
  2. You can ask us to suspend the use of your location temporarily.
  3. You can ask to see or be told about the records of use and provision of your location data.

6. Children

The Service is for adults only, so the provisions on guardians of children aged 8 and under do not apply.

In the app: to report or block someone, open the menu at the top right of their profile or your conversation.

How do I delete my account?

Tap Me › Account › Delete account. Your profile, photos, answers, links and conversations are deleted straight away. A deleted account cannot be restored.

When do photos become visible?

At first nobody can see them. Once a link is connected, you have exchanged at least six messages, and you both agree to share photos, you can see each other's.

Can other people see where I am?

No. We store your location only to about 1 km, and others see only an approximate distance such as “within 1 km” or “about 3 km”. You can turn it off any time under My location › Turn location off.

I received an upsetting message

Open the conversation menu, tap Report, and choose a reason. The other person is blocked immediately and is not told that you reported them. If you are in danger, contact your local emergency number first.

How do payments and refunds work?

Plus is billed through your App Store or Google Play account. Cancel under iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or in the Google Play app under Payments and subscriptions. Refunds are requested through Apple's “Report a Problem” page or Google Play's refund process.
